Manual of Experiment:

Aim: To analyze the performance of Transistor as CB configuration.

Apparatus Required:

Theory: The configuration in which the base of the transistor is common between emitter and collector circuit is called a common base configuration. In common base-emitter connection, the input is connected between emitter and base while the output is taken across collector and base.
