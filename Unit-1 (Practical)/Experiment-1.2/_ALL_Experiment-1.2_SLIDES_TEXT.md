### Slide 3
- Zener Diode
- Zener Diode is one of the most important semiconductor diodes used in our daily life. It is a specific diode that works in reverse bias conditions.
- A heavily doped p-n junction diode that works in reverse bias conditions is called a Zener Diode.

- Reverse Bias of Zener Diode
- In reverse voltage conditions a small amount of current flows through the Zener diode.
- This current is because of the electrons which are thermally generated in the Zener diode.
