### Slide 3
- PN Junction Diode
- The PN junction diode is a two-terminal device, which is formed when one side of the PN junction diode is made with p-type and doped with the N-type material.
- It operates in two modes forward bias and reverse bias.
- Figure-1 : PN Junction Diode [https://www.elprocus.com/vi-characteristics-of-pn-junction-diode/]
- 2

### Slide 4
- Forward Bias of PN Junction Diode
- When a PN-junction diode is connected in a forward bias by giving a positive voltage to the P-type material and a negative voltage to the N-type terminal.
- Additional / Recommended Readings:
- https://www.elprocus.com/vi-characteristics-of-pn-junction-diode/
- Figure.2 Forward Bias of PN Junction Diode
- 3

### Slide 5
- Reverse Bias of PN Junction Diode
- When a PN junction diode is connected in a Reverse Bias condition, a positive (+ Ve) voltage is connected to the N-type material & a negative (-Ve) voltage is connected to the P-type material.
- Additional / Recommended Readings:
- https://www.elprocus.com/vi-characteristics-of-pn-junction-diode/
