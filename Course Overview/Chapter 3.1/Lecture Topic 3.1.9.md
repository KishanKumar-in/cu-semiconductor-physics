Applications of Transformer:

## 1. Power System Applications

Transmission & Distribution: Step-up transformers increase voltage for long-distance transmission to reduce power loss; step-down transformers decrease voltage for safe residential or industrial use.

Grid Interconnection: Connects different parts of the electrical grid with different voltage levels

## 2. Domestic Applications

Adapters/Chargers: Mobile chargers and appliance adapters use small step-down transformers.

Doorbells & HVAC Systems: Use low-voltage transformers.

Tube lights & LED drivers: Contain transformers to supply required voltage.

## 3. Industrial Applications

Welding Machines: Use transformers to provide low-voltage, high-current output.

Furnaces: Transformers are used in electric arc furnaces for steel-making.

Motor Starters: Control voltage in starting motors smoothly.
