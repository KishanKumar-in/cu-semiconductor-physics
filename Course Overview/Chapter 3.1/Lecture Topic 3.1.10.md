# Lecture Topic 3.1.10

CO.5 To Understand the fundamental laws to solve practical engineering problems of semiconductors, transistors, electric circuits, and transformers.

Numerical Based on Transformer:

1. A 10 kVA transformer has an iron loss of 300 W and full-load copper loss of 400 W. Find the full-load efficiency at unity power factor.

2. A transformer has total losses of 600 W and an efficiency of 96%. Find the output power.

3. A transformer delivers 5 kW at an efficiency of 95%. Find the total loss.

4. A transformer operates on 230 V and delivers 12 V at the secondary. Find the turns ratio.

5. A transformer secondary voltage is 24 V and the load current is 2 A. Find the output power.

6. A transformer has 1000 primary turns and 250 secondary turns. Identify whether it is step-up or step-down and calculate the voltage ratio.
