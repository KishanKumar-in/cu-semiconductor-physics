### Course Information
# Course Information

The course Semiconductor Physics and Basic Electronic Circuits provides fundamental knowledge of semiconductor materials, electronic devices, and basic electrical circuit analysis. The course covers the concepts of intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, energy band theory, and charge carrier conduction. It includes detailed study of the PN junction diode, its formation, biasing, V-I characteristics, and applications, along with the Zener diode, Zener and avalanche breakdown mechanisms, and voltage regulation applications. The course also explains different types of rectifiers, including half-wave, full-wave, center-tapped, and bridge rectifiers, along with ripple factor and filter circuits. In the transistor section, students learn the construction and operation of Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT), including NPN and PNP transistors, transistor configurations such as CB, CE, and CC, and applications of transistors as amplifiers and switches. Basic circuit analysis techniques using Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL) and Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL) are introduced for solving electrical networks. The course further includes the study of transformers, covering construction, working principle, EMF equation, transformer losses, efficiency, and practical applications. Overall, the course develops theoretical understanding and practical skills in semiconductor devices and basic electronic circuits essential for electrical and electronics engineering students.

### Course Introduction, Objectives and Outcomes
# Course Introduction, Objectives and Outcomes

## Course Introduction:

In this subject students will learn basic fundamentals of semiconductor physics with its applications. Basically Semiconductor physics is the branch of physics that deals with the properties of semiconductor materials, especially how they conduct electricity under different conditions. Semiconductors are the backbone of modern electronics—used in computers, phones, solar cells, LEDs, and more.

In unit-1, we will focus on formation of pn junction diode with its characteristics and applications. they will focus on Basic Structure of P-N Junction, P-N Junction as a diode, Forward and reverse biasing of P-N Junction diode, Volt-Ampere Characteristics of P-N Junction Diode, Avalanche Breakdown,Zener Breakdown,Zener diode, Volt-ampere Characteristics of Zener Diode, Applications of P-N Junction and Zener diode. Further with the help of practical knowledge they will be able to design projects on semiconductor physics.

In unit-2, students will learn about transistor in which it contains Introduction to BJT: Formation of PNP / NPN junctions, Transistor Circuit Configuration: CB, CE, CC configuration ,Operating regions of Transistor, injection efficiency, base transport factor and current amplification factors, Transistor as a switch and an Amplifier. Similarly, another topic is Introduction to energy storage devices, Selecting Battery: Basic Battery Specifications, common parameters of battery/applications ,Different types of Batteries used in different applications, Power Supplies: Linear and SMPS.

In unit-3 students will learn about basic concepts of AC and DC circuits with applications of kirchhoff's laws. Introduction to AC circuits, Study of AC circuits of series RL, RC circuit corresponding Voltage-current Phasor diagram and their relation will be covered with basic construction and working principles of transformer.

## Course Objectives:

The course should enable the student to

1.      To describe the the basic materials and the fundamental properties of semiconductor materials, including intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, charge carriers, and PN junction behavior.

2.      To understand the construction, operation, and characteristics of Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs), FETs and its applications.

3.      To apply the knowledge of semiconductor devices, transistors, electric circuits, and transformers to solve practical engineering problems in electronics, communication, and power systems.

## Course Outcomes:

CO.1 Describe the properties of materials and Application of semiconductor electronics devices such as diodes, Transistor.

CO.2 Examine the characteristics and operaing principles of various electronic devices includes diodes and transistors.

CO.3 Apply the relationships between the key parameters of diode, transistor configurations, to analyze electronics circuits.

CO.4 Illustrate the characteristics of diodes, different types of transistors and the basic concept of electric circuits.

CO.5 To Understand the fundamental laws to solve practical engineering problems of semiconductors, transistors, electric circuits, and transformers.

### Netiquette Guidelines
# Netiquette Guidelines

Netiquette is etiquette for the internet, guidelines and good practices for online communication in emails, discussion forums, and chat rooms.

Please practice the following guidelines when posting comments in the course:

- Format your text so it is easy to read. Try to use standard paragraph spacing, indentation, and legible font. Try to be consistent with your formatting, including font size, color, etc.

- Be concise and to the point in your communication. Limit discussion forum posts to a few paragraphs. Proofread and edit your work before posting.

- AVOID THE USE OF ALL CAPS as this may be interpreted as shouting. All lowercase letters also make text more difficult to read. Use standard punctuation. Remember what your teacher taught you in primary school about good punctuation and spelling.

- Avoid 'texting' abbreviations such as OMG.

- Avoid using humor and sarcasm, as they are often difficult to detect in written communication. It is okay to sparingly use emoticons to communicate subtleties or emotions that are more obvious in spoken communication.

- Flaming is not proper in our courses (a rant about someone or some topic). Practice civil discussion.

- Respect the privacy of communication within a course. Do not publicly forward the comments of classmates to others outside of the course.

- Stay on topic in a discussion.

- Be respectful.

- Participants have varying degrees of sensitivity, and that even some well-meaning constructive suggestions (e.g., comments about broken links, typo, and misspellings) may be misconstrued as public criticism. Therefore, please refrain from sharing comments in ways that might be misinterpreted, or do so privately when appropriate.

(Any violation to the above points if reported by the teacher may lead to disciplinary action against the student)
