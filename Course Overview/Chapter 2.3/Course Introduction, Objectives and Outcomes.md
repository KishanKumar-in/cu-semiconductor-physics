# Course Introduction, Objectives and Outcomes

## Course Introduction:

In this subject students will learn basic fundamentals of semiconductor physics with its applications. Basically Semiconductor physics is the branch of physics that deals with the properties of semiconductor materials, especially how they conduct electricity under different conditions. Semiconductors are the backbone of modern electronics—used in computers, phones, solar cells, LEDs, and more.

In unit-1, we will focus on formation of pn junction diode with its characteristics and applications. they will focus on Basic Structure of P-N Junction, P-N Junction as a diode, Forward and reverse biasing of P-N Junction diode, Volt-Ampere Characteristics of P-N Junction Diode, Avalanche Breakdown,Zener Breakdown,Zener diode, Volt-ampere Characteristics of Zener Diode, Applications of P-N Junction and Zener diode. Further with the help of practical knowledge they will be able to design projects on semiconductor physics.

In unit-2, students will learn about transistor in which it contains Introduction to BJT: Formation of PNP / NPN junctions, Transistor Circuit Configuration: CB, CE, CC configuration ,Operating regions of Transistor, injection efficiency, base transport factor and current amplification factors, Transistor as a switch and an Amplifier. Similarly, another topic is Introduction to energy storage devices, Selecting Battery: Basic Battery Specifications, common parameters of battery/applications ,Different types of Batteries used in different applications, Power Supplies: Linear and SMPS.

In unit-3 students will learn about basic concepts of AC and DC circuits with applications of kirchhoff's laws. Introduction to AC circuits, Study of AC circuits of series RL, RC circuit corresponding Voltage-current Phasor diagram and their relation will be covered with basic construction and working principles of transformer.

## Course Objectives:

The course should enable the student to

1.      To describe the the basic materials and the fundamental properties of semiconductor materials, including intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, charge carriers, and PN junction behavior.

2.      To understand the construction, operation, and characteristics of Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs), FETs and its applications.

3.      To apply the knowledge of semiconductor devices, transistors, electric circuits, and transformers to solve practical engineering problems in electronics, communication, and power systems.

## Course Outcomes:

CO.1 Describe the properties of materials and Application of semiconductor electronics devices such as diodes, Transistor.

CO.2 Examine the characteristics and operaing principles of various electronic devices includes diodes and transistors.

CO.3 Apply the relationships between the key parameters of diode, transistor configurations, to analyze electronics circuits.

CO.4 Illustrate the characteristics of diodes, different types of transistors and the basic concept of electric circuits.

CO.5 To Understand the fundamental laws to solve practical engineering problems of semiconductors, transistors, electric circuits, and transformers.
